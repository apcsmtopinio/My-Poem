I Love Purple and Blue Red Red Red

Purple flowers dance in the breeze,
Their lovely colors charm the trees.
They sparkle softly in the sun,
A magical shade of dreams and fun.

Purple clouds fill the sky,
As little fairies flutter by.
A color full of joy and grace,
Like magic in a dreamland place.

Blue skies sparkle way up high,
Like little stars that fill the sky.
The ocean waves dance in the sun,
A lovely color full of fun.

Red roses bloom beneath the sun,
Their lovely petals shine with fun.
Red sunsets glow across the sky,
Like little flames that dance up high.
